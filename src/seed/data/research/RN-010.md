# ZYNX public comps imply high bar for post-readout rerating

Ticker: ZYNX

The public valuation debate around Zynx Therapeutics increasingly hinges on which peer set investors choose. High-quality immune-modulation stories still command premiums, but those premiums are reserved for assets with either cleaner late-stage validation or clearer commercial pathways than ZYNX has disclosed publicly. That does not make the stock unattractive; it means the hurdle for a lasting rerating is higher than recent momentum suggests.

We compare ZYNX against a basket of event-driven biotech names with similar cash runway and development concentration. On that basis, the shares do not look outright expensive, but they are no longer ignored. The market will want not just a positive signal, but a signal robust enough to support partnering economics, trial continuity, and manageable future dilution.

For public investors, the appeal is still asymmetry backed by liquidity. The risk is that a merely adequate update fails to satisfy a more demanding audience. We would frame the name as a catalyst-sensitive public idea rather than a core long-term conviction ahead of the data window.
