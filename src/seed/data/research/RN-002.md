# ACME aftermarket margin mix still underappreciated

Ticker: ACME

ACME is increasingly trading like a cyclical capital-goods supplier when nearly half of gross profit now comes from service, consumables, and retrofit work. Conversations with regional resellers point to elevated attachment rates on monitoring software and long-tail spare parts. Those items carry lower revenue visibility in public filings but materially improve earnings durability.

Our sum-of-the-parts view assigns a premium multiple to the service franchise and a market multiple to the project business. That still yields upside versus the current blended valuation. Public peers with comparable aftermarket intensity have historically defended margins better during PMI slowdowns, particularly when fleet age and compliance upgrades force maintenance spending. ACME appears to be following that pattern.

The main public risk is execution around a plant-consolidation program announced earlier this year. We think the market is pricing a more disruptive integration path than the company has telegraphed. If management keeps fill rates steady through the transition, the stock can rerate on better-than-feared operating leverage.
