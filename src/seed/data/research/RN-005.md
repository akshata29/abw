# MRDN deposit costs are peaking as remix stabilizes

Ticker: MRDN

MRDN has spent the past year repricing deposits faster than assets, compressing headline net interest margin and weighing on sentiment across the regional-bank group. Public disclosures, however, now suggest the remix pressure is easing. Non-interest-bearing outflows have moderated, promotional time-deposit campaigns are rolling off, and commercial customers appear less aggressive in sweeping idle cash into money-market products.

Credit also looks better than the market narrative implies. Office exposure remains manageable relative to capital, and recent reserve builds seem geared toward prudence rather than emerging stress. We do not dismiss the macro risk, but the bank’s public filings show a granular deposit base and limited reliance on rate-sensitive venture clients. That should allow MRDN to defend earnings even if the Fed stays on hold longer than expected.

The shares probably need a cleaner quarter before rerating meaningfully, yet valuation already prices in a harsher outcome than our base case. For investors comfortable with regional-bank volatility, we see improving asymmetry as funding pressure normalizes.
