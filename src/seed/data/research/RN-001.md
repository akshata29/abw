# ACME Q1 order book steadies despite softer OEM channels

Ticker: ACME

ACME enters the quarter with mixed end-market signals. Management commentary from distributors suggests replacement demand in factory automation stayed resilient even as original-equipment orders lengthened from six weeks to roughly nine. Our channel calls indicate backlog conversion, not fresh cancellation, explains most of the recent headline volatility. Pricing on service contracts remains positive and should offset a modest mix headwind from lower-margin components.

We model free-cash-flow conversion at the upper end of management guidance because working-capital unwind is tracking ahead of internal planning. The stock already discounts a shallow industrial slowdown, yet consensus still underappreciates the annuity value of the installed base and field-service franchise. In our framework, even flat organic growth can sustain margin resilience if utilization stays above prior-cycle averages.

Near-term catalysts are routine rather than transformative: earnings, a possible bolt-on maintenance acquisition, and updated medium-term targets at the fall investor day. We remain constructive on risk-reward for public-side investors and would use broad macro pullbacks to add, while acknowledging that export-sensitive verticals could stay noisy for another quarter.
