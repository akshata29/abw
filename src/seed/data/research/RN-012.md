# Cross-sector notebook: public cyclical breadth broadens beyond mega-cap tech

Ticker: ACME

Public-market breadth has improved modestly outside mega-cap technology, and that matters for several of our covered names. Industrial maintenance budgets, regional-bank funding anxiety, freight lane utilization, and energy service inflation all look incrementally better than they did two quarters ago. None of those datapoints screams boom; taken together, they suggest the macro backdrop for second-tier cyclicals is no longer deteriorating.

Within our fictional coverage set, ACME and GLBX appear best positioned to benefit from steadier operational execution, while MRDN and NTRX offer more valuation support if sentiment improves. ZYNX and VRDN remain catalyst-driven and should be sized accordingly. This note is not a sector call so much as a reminder that public-stock selection gets easier when breadth broadens and investors are willing to revisit neglected balance-sheet stories.

We continue to prefer public ideas with identifiable self-help levers over pure beta. In the current tape, modestly improving fundamentals combined with disciplined capital allocation can still outperform dramatic narratives that require perfect macro timing.
