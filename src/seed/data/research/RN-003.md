# GLBX freight tender data point to normalization, not recession

Ticker: GLBX

Spot and contract lane data for GLBX imply that North American freight is normalizing from an unsustainably weak base rather than deteriorating into a fresh recession. The company has tightened purchased-transport capacity, and brokerage commentary from public peers suggests bid discipline is finally returning on the most competitive lanes. That should support incremental margin recovery even if top-line growth remains modest.

We view warehousing and cross-border services as the underfollowed part of the story. Importers continue to rationalize inventories, but we see evidence that customers are consolidating vendors and awarding more bundled work to operators with dependable customs and visibility tooling. GLBX screens well on that dimension. It is not a rapid-growth narrative; it is a steady-share-gain narrative with improving asset turns.

The stock likely needs two more quarters of clean execution before public investors fully credit the turnaround. Still, downside looks bounded by tangible asset coverage and easing capex. We stay constructive, particularly for investors seeking a transport name with less earnings beta than pure-play truckload carriers.
