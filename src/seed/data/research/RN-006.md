# MRDN fee businesses cushion a slower rate-reset cycle

Ticker: MRDN

The public market still frames MRDN almost entirely as a balance-sheet duration story, but fee income is doing more to stabilize the model than most screens capture. Treasury-management wins, card fees, and wealth balances have held up well, and management continues to add commercial bankers in markets where branch density already supports low-cost gathering. That is not enough to eliminate funding pressure, but it does soften the path.

Our scenario work assumes only modest benefit from future rate cuts. Even under that conservative setup, MRDN can generate acceptable returns if charge-offs remain near through-cycle averages. Importantly, the bank is not signaling a willingness to stretch on credit or pricing to protect near-term earnings. That restraint should matter for credibility with investors after a period in which the whole group lost it.

Public catalysts include updated expense targets, another quarter of stable deposits, and any sign that classified assets are plateauing. We remain selective in the regional-bank space, but MRDN screens as a better-quality public vehicle than its headline multiple suggests.
